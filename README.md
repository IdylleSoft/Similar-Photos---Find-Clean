## ✨ A cleaner photo library, without the hard work

Over time, photo folders fill up with repeated downloads, burst shots, edited copies and nearly identical pictures. Deciding what to keep can take hours.

**Similar Photos - Find & Clean** brings related photos together, helps you spot the best one and makes cleanup feel simple and safe.

## 🌟 Smart Select

Not sure which photo deserves to stay?

Smart Select compares details such as clarity, resolution and lighting, then keeps its best suggestion while selecting the alternatives for you. You always remain in control and can change the selection before cleaning.

Optional face checking can also help identify moments with closed eyes, less-sharp faces, awkward framing or people looking away.

## 🛡️ Your memories stay yours

Your photos are analyzed directly on your computer. They are not uploaded for photo comparison, and face checking runs entirely on your device.

Cleanup is designed to be reassuring:

- Nothing is removed without your confirmation
- At least one photo is kept in every group
- Selecte1d files are checked again before cleanup
- Removed photos go to the Windows Recycle Bin
